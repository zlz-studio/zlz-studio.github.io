# v2.3.0 — Halftone Pattern & 2 Shadows

**Latest version**

### New Features

- Added **2 Shadows** to [ToonRampShade](/features/ToonRampSmooth/): a second, deeper shadow step with its own color
- Added **[Halftone Pattern](/features/Halftone/)**: shading drawn as dots, lines, cross-hatch or your own pattern texture

### Changed

- **Shadow Color** moved from Base Colors into ToonRampShade, next to the new shadow controls
- The included **Face Shadow** texture now carries the second shadow in its B / A channels. Custom face textures keep working with 1 Shadow
